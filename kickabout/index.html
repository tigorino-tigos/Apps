How to play

Keyboard

WASD or arrows
Run (and aim passes and shots)
X or Space
Pass to the team-mate you are facing. Without the ball: switch player
Z hold, release
Shoot: hold longer for more power. Up or down picks the corner
C
Sliding tackle
Shift
Sprint (watch the stamina ring)
Q
Switch to the player nearest the ball
Esc or P
Pause
M
Sound on or off

Touch

Drag anywhere on the left half to run. The buttons on the right pass, shoot (hold for power), tackle and sprint. Pass becomes Switch when the other side has the ball.

Gamepad

Left stick runs. A passes, B shoots (hold), X tackles, Y or LB switches, RB or RT sprints, Start pauses.

Tips

The ring under your player shows stamina. A faint ring marks who your pass will find. Keepers can't be tackled with the ball in their own area.
