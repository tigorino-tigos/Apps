Tigos Drill Board

Privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

The Tigos Drill Board is a planning app for football and multi-sport coaches. It is made by Tigos (“we”). This page explains what the app keeps, where, and why. You can ask us anything about it at tigosproductions@gmail.com.

The short version

  • Without an account, everything you do stays on your own device. We never see it.
  • With an account, we keep your email address, the name you choose, your team, the sessions you share and a record of the sessions you run (for XP and the leaderboard).
  • The app is for coaches, who are adults. It never asks for children’s names or details; teams in a tournament are known only by their bib colour.
  • No adverts, no tracking across other apps or websites, no analytics, and we never sell data.
  • You can delete your account, and everything kept with it, at any time inside the app.

Kept on your device only

Your favourites, history, ratings and notes, My session, the Board, tournaments and your settings are saved in the app’s storage on your phone or tablet. They are not sent to us. Deleting the app, or clearing its data, removes them.

If you sign in

Signing in is optional. It lets you share sessions with your team, copy other coaches’ sessions, earn XP and appear on the leaderboard. When you sign in we keep:

  • Your email address, to send you a sign-in link or code. We do not use it for marketing.
  • Your display name, shown on the sessions you share and on the leaderboard.
  • Your team, if you create or join one, and its name and colour.
  • Sessions you make, with who can see each one (only you, your team, or everyone).
  • Activity: when you run a session or drill, make a session or copy one, used to work out XP, your level and the leaderboard.

We use this only to run these features. Our legal basis is providing the service you asked for (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(b)).

Who else handles it

  • Supabase stores account data and sends sign-in emails for us, under a data processing agreement. Access is limited by database rules so each coach can only change their own data.
  • Google Fonts: the web version loads its fonts from Google, which receives your device’s IP address when it does. The App Store version carries its own fonts and does not.
  • Apple handles any payment for a subscription. We are told only whether a subscription is active, never your card details.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account.

Deleting your account

In the app: Profile → Delete my account. This deletes your login, profile, the sessions you shared and your activity straight away. A team you created passes to another coach in it so it carries on for them. Sessions saved on your device stay there until you delete the app. You can also ask us to do it by email.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, to correct it or to delete it, by emailing tigosproductions@gmail.com. If you are unhappy with how we handle it you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too, and anything important is shown in the app.
