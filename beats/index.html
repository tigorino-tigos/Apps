            Settings

            Faster notes are further apart, which can make busy songs easier to read.

            If your hits feel late (common with Bluetooth headphones), calibrate, or move this right.

            How to play

            Hit

            Notes slide down four lanes. Press the lane's key as a note crosses the bright line.

            DFJK or ←↓↑→. On a phone, tap the lanes. Gamepads work too.

            Hold

            Gems with a ring have a trail behind them. Keep holding until the trail ends.

            Score

            Perfect, Great and Good are about how close you were. Every ten in a row adds to your multiplier, up to ×2. Forty in a row starts the Fever.

            Make your own

            In Create, build a beat on the grid, or press Surprise me. Then Play as level, or share it as a link.

            Calibrate timing

            Tap any key, or tap the screen, on every click. Eight clicks after a count of four.
