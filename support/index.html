Signing in

Type your email and tap the link in the email we send, or type the code from it. No passwords. On iPhone, open the email on the same phone. If it doesn’t arrive, check your junk folder, then tap Send a new one.

The app shows an old version

Close the app completely and open it again. It updates itself the next time it opens.

Deleting your account

Profile → Delete my account. See the privacy policy for what this removes.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. To use a subscription on a new phone, tap Restore purchases in the app.
